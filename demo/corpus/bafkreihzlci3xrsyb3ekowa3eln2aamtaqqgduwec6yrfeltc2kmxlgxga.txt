Riverlight Library is a neighborhood reading and lending center. Riverlight Library is located in Cedar Square. Riverlight Library was founded by Asha Patel. Riverlight Library opened on 14 March 2024. Riverlight Library has 4,200 books. Riverlight Library charges ₹300 per year for adult membership. Riverlight Library opens at 9:00 AM on weekdays. Riverlight Library closes at 6:00 PM on weekdays. Riverlight Library provides a wheelchair ramp to make the entrance accessible. To register for membership, bring a photo ID and complete the form at the library desk.
